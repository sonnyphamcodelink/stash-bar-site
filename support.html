Stash Bar – Quick Notes for macOS

Contact

To report a problem or ask a question, open an issue on GitHub. Please include your macOS version and the steps that led to the problem.

Getting started

Stash Bar runs in the menu bar and has no Dock icon. Click its icon in the menu bar to open the pad. Press Escape or click outside to close it; your draft is saved automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How do I keep a note? Click Save to Stack. The note moves to your stack and a fresh draft opens. Open the stack to search, reopen, or move notes to Trash.

How do I back up a note? Use Export to save it as an RTFD document with its text and images, anywhere you choose.

How do I open Settings or quit? Right-click (or Control-click) the menu bar icon and choose Settings or Quit.

Where are my notes stored? Only on your Mac, inside the app's sandbox container. Nothing is synced or uploaded.
