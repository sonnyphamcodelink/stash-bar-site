Privacy Policy

Stash Bar – Quick Notes · Effective October 30, 2026

Summary

Stash Bar does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you write or paste stays on your Mac.

Data stored on your device

Your notes, including text and pasted images, and your app preferences (such as appearance and panel size) are stored locally in the app's sandboxed container on your Mac. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party. Deleting the app removes this data.

No accounts, analytics, or tracking

The app has no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, and no network features. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Clipboard and files

The app reads the clipboard only when you choose Paste. It writes files only when you choose Export and pick a location in the save panel.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
